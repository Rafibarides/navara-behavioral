Why Navara Pathways

Depth, not decks.

Most prep teaches you to recognize terms.

The exam asks you to make decisions.

Every Navara question puts you inside a case: a learner, a dataset, a supervisee, an ethical gray area. You choose what a competent BCBA would do next.

Then you see why, in plain language, with every wrong option explained too - because that's where the learning is.

Guided

Some questions deserve more than another attempt.

You can reread an explanation. You can reconsider an answer. But sometimes what you want is to talk through your reasoning with someone who understands behavior analysis. Guided adds two 45-minute one-to-one sessions every month with an NCBS™ - dedicated time for the material you find hardest.

✔

Ninety minutes a month, on your questions

Talk through concepts you haven't fully understood, examine questions where two options seem equally convincing, and review the topics you want to strengthen.

✔

Practice between sessions

Your sessions complement the independent work inside the platform. Bring the questions you want to discuss, then keep moving with the bank and timed sets.

✔

An option, not an obligation

Prefer to work independently? Self-Study gives you the full practice toolkit. Guided adds the dedicated one-to-one time.
