The Navara Behavior Method™

A 12-week program that uses four phases to train YOU, the parent, to think like a BCBA and a developmental psychologist.

1
Connection
Weeks 1-3

Think like a developmental psychologist. Calm and trust first, because strategy fails a dysregulated child.

2
Clarity
Weeks 4-7

Think like a behavior analyst. Learn what your child’s behavior is really telling you.

3
Control
Weeks 8-10

Put your plan to work in the home you actually have, with support.

4
Carryover
Weeks 11-12

Make it last: school, grandparents, and the bad weeks.

About Navara

Clinical depth. A more personal kind of care.

Navara Behavioral Group began with a conviction: families deserve support that makes sense in the life they actually live. Behavioral science should help parents understand their child, make clear decisions, and respond with confidence. It should not leave them with a plan that only makes sense in a session.

The founder

Built from experience. Guided by connection.

David Nisan Fetman, MA, BCBA, known as Nisan, founded Navara after nearly a decade in behavioral healthcare. A Board Certified Behavior Analyst, clinical leader, and psychology doctoral candidate, he brings together behavioral science, clinical psychology, and a practical understanding of family life.

He is pursuing a doctorate in psychology and a second master's degree in clinical psychopharmacology, while working toward the BCBA-D designation. That depth of training supports a simple conviction: connection comes before compliance.

The vision

Close the gap between expertise and everyday life.

A family can receive sound clinical advice and still be left asking, "What do we do when this happens at home?" Navara was founded to close that gap. Our vision is to place parents at the center of care, bring clarity to complex behavior, and turn clinical understanding into practical support for daily life.

We are building a practice around the family, rather than asking the family to fit a standard model.
